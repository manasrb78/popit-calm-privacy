Support

Found a bug, or have an idea to make Popit Calm calmer? Here is how to reach us and what usually fixes things.

Report a problem or share an idea: open an issue. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app).

Quick fixes

  • The app misbehaves. Force-quit and reopen it. Update to the latest iOS and app version.
  • No sound. Check the silent switch on your phone and the Sound toggle in Settings inside the app.
  • A round paused by itself. That is intentional: calls, switching apps and the Control Center pause the round.
  • I can't find the Popit Calm filter in Focus settings. Open Popit Calm once first, then look again under Settings → Focus → your Focus → Add Filter.
  • Notifications still arrive. Check the Focus mode is on (Control Center → Focus) and that the apps you want quiet are not in its allowed list.
  • I want to reset my scores. In the app, open Settings and tap Reset my progress. This cannot be undone.
  • A tip didn't go through. If you were charged, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tips are handled entirely by Apple and unlock nothing.

Setting up Focus mode

  1. Settings → Focus → + → Custom, and name it “Popit Calm”.
  2. Under Apps, allow only the apps you need (no social media).
  3. Add Filter → Popit Calm.
  4. Optional: Shortcuts → Automation → App → Popit Calm opens → Set Focus: Popit Calm.

iOS does not let apps block other apps' notifications, so Focus does the muting. The same steps are in the app under Settings → Focus setup.
