Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Popit Calm does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your scores, streaks, play time, weekly focus history and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No network connection is needed or used by the game. Only the optional tip jar (below) talks to Apple's App Store

Sharing your score

If you tap “Share my score”, the standard iOS share sheet opens with a score card image and a short caption. Only what you choose to send, to the person or app you choose, is shared. We do not see it.

Tips

Popit Calm has an optional tip jar in Settings. Tips are one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details, and a tip unlocks nothing: the game is the same for everyone.

Focus mode

Popit Calm offers an optional Focus Filter. It only records, on your device, that a Focus session is active. The app cannot read your notifications or the content of other apps.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
